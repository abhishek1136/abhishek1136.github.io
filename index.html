WHOOP WhatsApp AI Coach

A personal AI fitness coach that lives in WhatsApp. Every morning at 7 AM it automatically syncs biometric data from WHOOP — recovery score, HRV, sleep stages, strain, and workout history — and sends an intelligent coaching briefing. The agent learns from every conversation: corrections, goals, injuries, and preferences are stored permanently and baked into every future response.

01 · Sync
Daily cron pulls incremental WHOOP data into SQLite. API called once per day only.
02 · Store
SQLite DB stores snapshots, workouts, conversation history, and learned intelligence.
03 · Coach
WhatsApp message → FastAPI → Claude reads DB → personalised coaching response.
04 · Learn
Every correction and goal is detected and saved. Agent gets smarter over time.
